书签小屋 · Bookmark House

隐私政策 / Privacy policy

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 11 日 · 适用于书签小屋 Chrome 扩展

简体中文 · English

书签小屋用于保存、回顾和整理网页收藏与浏览器书签。收藏内容保存在当前设备、当前 Chrome 个人资料的扩展空间中。开发者不接收你的收藏，不提供收藏上传服务器，也不使用广告、第三方统计或远程模型服务。

1. 扩展处理哪些信息

  • 浏览器书签的网址、标题、文件夹结构，以及 Chrome 提供的添加或最近使用时间（如果可用），用于书签库展示、搜索、分类、移动、编辑、恢复和导出。
  • 你主动保存或导入的网页网址、标题、正文摘句、本地生成的摘要、站点名称、作者和发布日期，以及你填写的备注、星标、阅读状态和收藏日期。
  • 你选择保留的本地预览图片。使用工具栏的封面选项时，扩展可以保存当前可见页面的缩略截图；部分页面也可复用已加载的文章图片。截图或文章内容可能包含页面上当时可见的个人信息，请按需选择保存。
  • 侧栏宽度、显示与语言偏好、缓存索引、操作撤销记录、回收站副本和最近一次清理前备份，以及用于避免反复读取失败页面的重试记录。

扩展不读取密码管理器或浏览器的全部历史记录，不专门采集表单输入、登录凭据或支付信息。用户自行保存的页面正文或备注仍可能包含个人信息，这些内容按收藏数据在本地处理。

2. 这些信息如何使用

信息仅用于本扩展的收藏与书签管理功能，包括内容展示、搜索、主题分析、生成正文摘要、目录整理、备份和恢复。摘要与主题识别使用随扩展一起安装的本地规则，不向外部模型发送正文。页面加载计时也只在当前弹窗中显示，不发送给开发者。

3. 哪些情况下会访问网站

通过工具栏主动保存网页时，扩展使用临时的当前标签页权限读取你选择的主页面。为已保存链接补充或更新内容时，扩展会按站点请求可选访问权限。站点获准后，扩展可能在你展示这些收藏时读取该站点已保存页面，或读取你已打开的对应页面，以补充缺失内容。

直接读取公开网页及同站封面的请求发往你选择的网站，不经由开发者服务器；这些请求省略 Cookie、不发送来源网址，也不自动跟随跳转。原网站可能按自身政策收到常规网络信息，如请求的网址和网络地址。读取已打开页面时，可以读取你当时已登录后显示的内容，但不会提取或传送登录凭据。

打开收藏链接会按正常浏览方式访问相应网站，适用该网站自己的隐私规则。

4. 数据保存与共享

收藏、图片、备注与扩展内部回收站保存在本机 IndexedDB；布局与语言偏好保存在本机浏览器存储；语言选择也保存在本机数据库中，供后台菜单使用。开发者不出售、不出租这些数据，不将它们用于广告、跨站跟踪、信用或贷款决策，也不向其他主体提供收藏数据。

你可以主动导出 JSON 或 HTML 备份，并自行决定如何保存和分享这些文件。书签库直接管理 Chrome 的真实书签；如果你开启了 Chrome 书签同步，相关书签更改可能由 Chrome 同步到其他设备，适用 Google 的相关政策。扩展内部网页卡片和回收站不会通过本扩展同步到其他设备。

5. 保留、修改与删除

你可以在小屋中查看、修改、导出和删除收藏。删除项先进入内部回收站，回收站不会按时间自动清空。手动永久清空前，扩展会保存最近一次清理前备份；该备份会在后续清理时替换，或在扩展本地数据被删除时移除。整理操作也可能保留本地恢复记录。

卸载扩展会删除对应扩展空间中的本地收藏和缓存，但不会删除 Chrome 中原有的书签、已导出的文件或 Chrome 同步保留的其他副本。重要收藏请先导出备份。开发者无法远程查看或代为删除你设备中的收藏数据。

6. 权限说明

  • bookmarks：管理浏览器书签和目录，支持整理、备份与恢复。
  • activeTab：在你主动使用保存入口时，临时访问当前选中的网页。
  • scripting：运行扩展内置的正文和元信息读取函数。
  • contextMenus：提供网页和链接的右键收藏入口。
  • 可选网站权限：按站点授权，补充已保存页面的内容；可在 Chrome 扩展设置中撤销。

扩展不下载或执行远程代码。通过 Google / Chrome 接口获得的信息，仅用于本政策披露的收藏与书签管理功能，并遵守 Chrome Web Store 用户数据政策及其有限使用（Limited Use）要求。

7. 本政策网页与更新

本政策网页不包含广告、第三方统计脚本或收集表单。访问公开政策页时，网页托管服务会处理提供网页所需的常规网络信息。政策如有变更，会在本页更新生效日期；涉及功能和权限的变化也会随扩展版本说明披露。

8. 联系开发者

如有隐私或功能问题，请联系：leeguandon@gmail.com。请勿发送密码或其他不必要的敏感信息。

Privacy policy · English

Effective November 11, 2026 · Bookmark House Chrome extension

Bookmark House saves, revisits and organizes webpage collections and browser bookmarks. Collection data is kept on this device, in the current Chrome profile's extension storage. The developer does not receive your collection, operate a collection-upload server, or use advertising, third-party analytics or remote model services.

1. Information the extension handles

  • Bookmark URLs, titles, folder structure and available creation or last-used timestamps supplied by Chrome, for display, search, classification, moving, editing, recovery and export.
  • URLs, titles, text excerpts, locally generated summaries, site names, authors and publication dates for pages you save or import, together with your notes, stars, reading status and save dates.
  • Local preview images you choose to keep. The toolbar's cover option may save a thumbnail of the visible webpage; some pages may reuse an already-loaded article image. Screenshots or article content may contain personal information visible on the page. Choose whether to save them accordingly.
  • Sidebar width, display preferences, interface language, cache indexes, undo records, Trash copies, the most recent pre-cleanup backup, and retry records that avoid repeatedly requesting unavailable pages.

The extension does not read your password manager or complete browser history, and does not specifically collect form input, login credentials or payment information. Page text and notes that you choose to save may still contain personal information; they are handled locally as collection data.

2. How this information is used

Information is used only for collection and bookmark management: display, search, topic analysis, text excerpts, folder organization, backups and recovery. Summaries and topics use local rules packaged with the extension. Text is not sent to an external model. Page-load timing is displayed only in the current popup and is not sent to the developer.

3. When websites are accessed

When you actively save a page from the toolbar, the extension uses temporary active-tab access to read the selected main page. To supplement or update saved links, it requests optional access per website. Once a website is authorized, the extension may read saved pages from that site when you display those saves, or read a matching page you already have open, to fill in missing content.

Direct requests for public webpages and same-site covers go to the website you selected, without a developer-operated relay. These requests omit cookies and referrers and do not automatically follow redirects. The website may receive normal network information under its own policy, including the requested URL and network address. Reading an already-open page may include content visible in your signed-in session, but does not extract or transmit login credentials.

Opening a saved link is normal browsing governed by the corresponding website's privacy rules.

4. Storage and sharing

Collections, images, notes and the extension's internal Trash are kept in local IndexedDB. Layout and language preferences are kept in local browser storage; the language preference is also available to the extension worker. The developer does not sell or rent this data, use it for advertising, cross-site tracking, credit or lending decisions, or provide collection data to another party.

You can export JSON or HTML backups and choose how to store or share them. The bookmark library directly manages Chrome's native bookmarks. If Chrome bookmark sync is enabled, Chrome may synchronize those changes to other devices under Google's policies. This extension does not synchronize its webpage cards or internal Trash to other devices.

5. Retention, editing and deletion

You can view, edit, export and delete saves in Bookmark House. Deleted items first enter internal Trash, which does not expire automatically. Before permanent clearing, the extension retains the most recent pre-cleanup backup. This backup is replaced by a later cleanup backup or removed when local extension data is deleted. Organization actions may also retain local recovery records.

Uninstalling removes the corresponding extension's local collection and caches. It does not remove Chrome's native bookmarks, exported files or other copies retained by Chrome sync. Export important saves first. The developer cannot remotely view or delete collection data on your device.

6. Permissions

  • bookmarks: manage browser bookmarks and folders, including organization, backup and recovery.
  • activeTab: temporary access to the selected webpage when you actively use a save entry.
  • scripting: run the extension's packaged text and metadata extraction functions.
  • contextMenus: provide a save action in webpage and link context menus.
  • Optional site permissions: supplement saved webpage content after authorization per website. Access can be revoked in Chrome's extension settings.

The extension does not download or execute remote code. Information received through Google or Chrome APIs is used only for the collection-management purpose disclosed here and complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

7. Policy website and updates

The public policy website has no advertising, third-party analytics scripts or collection forms. Its hosting provider processes normal delivery information when the policy page is visited. Policy changes update the effective date on that page; changes to functions or permissions are also disclosed in extension release notes. The Chinese and English versions describe the same data practices.

8. Contact

For privacy or product questions, email leeguandon@gmail.com. Do not send passwords or other unnecessary sensitive information.
